# Leads

What a visitor asks about a car on its public page, and how the salesperson hears of it (#18). A lead is a question ("Ask about this car") or a test drive request ("Book a test drive"). The module requires `staff` (who is signed in, the users and their roles, the staff page frame), `settings` (the dealership's opening hours and name, #22), `inventory` (the vehicles' entity type, `ListedVehicle` and the database's today) and `customers` (the customers' entity type, to link a lead by email). `storefront` requires it: the forms sit on a car's page, which only storefront can find by its slug, so storefront hosts the two posts and hands them to this module's exported `LeadDesk`.

## Data

`CreateLeadsTable` makes `leads`: an identity `id`; `vehicle_id`, whose foreign key cascades, so a car's leads go with it as its photos do (a car is deleted only before it is sold); `kind` (`inquiry` or `test_drive`); `name`, `email` (lower case), `phone` (empty when a question gives none) and `message`; `drive_on` and `drive_at` (`HH:MM`), null for a question; `salesperson_id` and `customer_id`, each null for none and set null when that user or customer is removed; `status` (`open` or `closed`); and `created_at`. It is indexed on `status` and on `vehicle_id`.

`AddLeadStage` (#30) adds `stage`, a `LeadStage` (`new`, `contacted`, `appointment`, `won`, `lost`), indexed. AIC adds a column to a table with rows only as nullable, and its alteration writes the add before the retype, so the retype's `USING` fills every stored lead from its status: open is New, closed Lost. `status` stays, written from the stage on every write (`open` for New, Contacted and Appointment, `closed` for Won and Lost), so the list and everything else that reads it is as it was. `CreateLeadMovesTable` makes `lead_moves`: an identity `id`, `lead_id` (cascading with the lead), `from_stage`, `to_stage`, `user_id` (who moved it, set null when that user goes) and `created_at`, one row per change of stage.

`Entity/LeadTable` is its entity type, referring to inventory's, staff's and customers' exported types. `LeadRepository` records, finds, lists, reassigns and closes leads, and reads the salespeople, all through AIC's `EntityStore`.

## Assignment

The salespeople are the users whose role is `salesperson`, less those an admin deactivated (#22), who take no new lead and are no choice for a reassignment; a visitor's mail is signed with the dealership's name from the profile. `LeadRepository::record()` runs one `EntityStore::transaction()`: it locks the salespeople's rows ordered by id (`forUpdate`), reads the newest lead's salesperson, takes the next one by id (the first when there is none or past the last), and inserts the lead. A second lead at the same moment waits for the lock and sees the first, so leads go to each in turn. With no salesperson the lead is unassigned. Since the turn follows the newest lead, removing leads brings it back: gate:23's two passes, each deleting its car and the car's leads, assign alike.

A lead whose email is a customer's (both lower case) is linked to that customer.

## The public forms

`LeadDesk::panels()` gives a car's page its two panels, the theme's `Panel` keyed `inquiry` and `test-drive` and anchored `car-inquiry` and `car-test-drive`, each holding AIC's `Form` (with the form guard's token and honeypot, so a visitor who never signed in gets a session for the token). Each form ends with the theme's `SubmitButton`, whose `data-target` names its own panel's anchor: Tessera reads a submit's target from the button first, so it swaps that panel alone. `LeadDesk::send()` checks the post (`LeadChecks`, with `LeadHours` for a test drive), and answers both panels with 422 and the errors beside their fields, or records the lead, queues its job and answers 200 with a thank-you (the theme's `Notice`) where the form was. Tessera's write id makes a double click record one lead.

A question needs a name, an email and the question; its phone is optional. A test drive needs a phone, a day after the database's today that the dealership is open, and a time from its opening to an hour before its closing. The hours are the dealership's profile, which `settings`' exported `DealershipProfiles` gives (#22): open Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 18:00, until an admin saves others, so a drive starts from 09:00 to 17:00. `LeadHours` builds its errors and the form's line from the hours (`OpeningHours`' days in words, its last start an hour before closing), so they read as before with the defaults. Days and times compare as text with `strcmp()`, and the weekday is worked out in integers, so no time zone enters it.

## Notifications

Recording a lead queues one `lead-notices` job with the lead's id; the request sends no mail. `SendLeadNotices` reads the lead (one that is gone, with its car, is done with nothing sent) and `LeadNotices` sends through AIC's `Notifier`, which queues one `notify` job per channel: the salesperson's notification (their user id, the visitor's details, and the lead's page `/app/leads/{id}` as its link, which the inbox's opening goes on to, #19) to `database` (`aic_notifications`) and `mail`, and the visitor's confirmation to `mail` alone. One `bin/aic queue:work` runs them all, since the worker takes jobs until none is due. In DDEV the mail lands in Mailpit; on this box only the web container reaches Mailpit's SMTP, so run the worker with `ddev exec bin/aic queue:work`.

## Staff pages

| Route | Controller | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /app/leads` | `LeadListPage` | With `view=board`, the board. Otherwise the open leads, newest first: at most 50 with how many match, filtered by `kind` and `salesperson` (an email); an unknown value of either shows every lead. The day a lead came in shows without its time. |
| `GET /app/leads/{id}` | `LeadPage` | The lead and its car (linked to its inventory page), its salesperson and its customer; the reassign and close forms while it is open, for its salesperson or a manager. 404 for no lead. |
| `POST /app/leads/{id}/assign` | `LeadAssignPost` | Gives the lead to the salesperson the form names by email: 303 to the lead with a notice; 422 for one who is not a salesperson. |
| `POST /app/leads/{id}/close` | `LeadClosePost` | Closes the lead, a move to Lost: 303 to the list with a notice. |
| `PATCH /app/leads/{id}/stage` | `LeadStagePatch` | Moves the lead to the posted `stage`: the board, 200, which AIC answers with the columns the action names; 422, 403 or 404. |
| `POST /app/leads/{id}/stage` | `LeadStagePost` | The same move without script: 303 to the board with a notice. |
| `GET /app/leads/events` | `LeadBoardEvents` | The board's stream. |

Only the lead's salesperson and a manager (an admin or a sales manager) may reassign or close it (`LeadAccess`): anyone else gets 403 and sees no forms. A closed lead answers 409; an unknown one 404. The sidebar's "Leads" link opens the list.

## The board

`GET /app/leads?view=board` is the board (#30): the list's route and panel, with AIC's `Tabs` ("Lead views", List and Board) at the top of both, so a tab swaps the content region in place. The links are not under `/app/leads/`, so gate:23's lead captures keep taking the first lead. Five columns, New, Contacted, Appointment, Won and Lost, each the theme's `BoardColumn` keyed by its stage and anchored `leads-<stage>`, built from a `BoardColumnSource`, so an answer naming two columns reads those two alone. A column shows its count and its newest 50 cards (`LeadCard`).

A card shows the visitor, the car, the kind, the salesperson and the day. For whoever may move the lead (`BoardViewer`, the rule `LeadAccess` holds: its salesperson or a manager) it is draggable and holds a "Move to" menu, a details element, with one form per other stage, each posting `stage` to `/app/leads/{id}/stage`. With script each button sends `@patch` with its form and a target of the card's column and the stage's column, by their anchors; a click outside the menu closes it (`data-on:click__outside`). A drag sets the board's region signals `dragAction` (the card's move route), `dragTail` (`-<id>`, which ends each of its forms' ids, `lead-to-<stage>-<id>`) and `dragFrom` (its column), and the column it drops on sends the dragged card's own form for its stage. Every id on a card ends with the lead's id (`lead-menu-<id>`, `lead-move-<id>`, `lead-to-<stage>-<id>`, `lead-go-<stage>-<id>`), and the drag writes it only as `-<id>` or inside its path: gate:23 masks the captured lead's id after a `-` and before no word character or `-`. Tessera's expressions read no `dataTransfer`, and Firefox starts no drag without `setData()`, so drag works in Chromium and the menu everywhere. While the move runs its button carries `data-loading` and the card is dimmed; once it lands, the moved card's summary takes focus (`data-init="el.focus()"`, since Tessera moves no focus after an action) and `data-focus-key="lead-<id>"` names it for Back and Forward.

`StageMoves` is the one move: in one `EntityStore::transaction()` it locks the lead's row (`LeadMoveRepository::moveLocked()`), writes its stage and status, records the move and, for Won with a salesperson, queues `lead-won`. A move to the stage the lead is at writes nothing. The `PATCH` answers the board page, which AIC answers with the two columns the action named; the `POST` (no script) answers 303 to the board with "Moved the lead from <name> to <Stage>.". A stage that is none answers 422, a lead that is not there 404, someone else's 403. The lead page's close is a move to Lost through it. `SendLeadWon` reads the lead: still Won and assigned, it sends the salesperson an inbox notification (`LeadNotices::won()`, the database channel), linking to the lead.

`GET /app/leads/events` is the board's stream (`LeadBoardEvents`, `BoardStreams`, `BoardSource`), which the board opens with `data-stream`. A fresh connection sends every column, so a move made between the page and the connection still reaches it; one with `Last-Event-ID` sends the columns the moves after it touched. It then looks once a second for twenty looks and ends with `reconnect()`. Each patch is `inner`, the column's anchor its target and its `BoardStack` the component, since AIC renders a stream's component with no marks and Tessera's component `morph` wants them; the last patch of a look carries the newest move's id. The page names no move id: gate:23 compares each site's page, and the ids differ between its passes.

## Checks

gate:23's steps post a question and a test drive about the gate's own car, refused (a bad field, no token, the honeypot, a Sunday evening) and sent; reach the lead's page through the list with `"capture": "/app/leads/"` (AIC #200); reassign and close it, refused and done. The board's steps read it and its stream before the pass's first move, then move the inquiry by `POST` and by a Tessera `PATCH` and leave it Lost. The browser looks are `tests/browser/leads-*.spec.ts`, the board's `leads-board*.spec.ts`; the notification look runs the worker through `ddev exec` and reads Mailpit at the address `ddev describe -j` gives.
