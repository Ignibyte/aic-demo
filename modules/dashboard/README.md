# Dashboard

`/app`, the first page signed-in staff see (#8): the greeting in the header, this month's figures as stat tiles, the sales recorded last, and three charts drawn as SVG on the server. Tessera refreshes the tiles and the latest sales from the page itself every five seconds (#12). The module requires `customers`, `inventory`, `sales` and `staff`. It owns that one route, four report views and no table, command or export; it sits under `/app`, which the staff module's protected prefix keeps to signed-in staff, and the page is a `StaffPages` document.

## The page

`GET /app` (`DashboardPage`) reads the member from `StaffSession` (303 to `/sign-in` when the session holds none) and hands `StaffPages` the greeting, "Welcome, {name}", as the top bar's title, "{role} at Ridgeway Motors" as the line under it, and `Dashboard` as the document's title (`Dashboard · Lotline`), since the greeting names the member, not the page. Under the header, `content` holds the live part, keyed `live`, and then the `PanelGrid` of the charts, keyed `sales`, `makes` and `salespeople`. Each part is an AIC `SourcedComponent` inside its `KeyedComponent` (#14, AIC #192): `DashboardLive` is the live part's source and `DashboardChart` each chart's, and each reads its figures only in `build()`, which the render pass calls when it renders that part, so a Tessera answer for one part reads that part's figures alone. Each chart is anchored `dashboard-<key>` (`dashboard-makes`), so a request names one without a path.

- the theme's `LiveParts` `live`, built by `DashboardLive`, which Tessera polls (see [Live updates](#live-updates)), holding, keyed `tiles` and `feed`:
  - the theme's `StatTiles` `dashboard-tiles` ("This month on the lot"), built by `DashboardTiles`: `tile-in-stock`, the vehicles in stock, with how many more are on hold; `tile-sales` and `tile-revenue`, this month's sales and the sum of their prices, each with last month's under it; and `tile-days`, the average days the vehicles not sold have been on the lot, rounded, with how many vehicles that covers;
  - the wide panel `feed`, "Latest sales", built by `DashboardFeed`: the `DataTable` `sales-feed` (keyed `list`), a row for each of the five sales recorded last, the latest first, by id: the day, linked to the sale's page; the vehicle's title; the customer; the salesperson; and the price;
- the wide panel `sales`, "Sales by month": the theme's `ColumnChart` `sales-chart`, a column for each of the last twelve months, the oldest first, labelled by its short name with the year under the first month and under each January;
- the panel `makes`, "Inventory by make": the theme's `BarChart` `makes-chart`, a bar for each make of the vehicles in stock or on hold, the most first, then by make;
- the panel `salespeople`, "Revenue by salesperson": the `BarChart` `salespeople-chart`, a bar for each member of staff with a sale in the twelve months, their prices summed in whole dollars, the most first, then by name.

`DashboardCharts` builds the panels and hands the theme's `ColumnCharts` and `BarCharts` the labels and whole numbers; the theme lays the charts out and its templates draw them, colours from the stylesheet's classes. Each SVG is labelled by its title element and its description element, which holds the summary `ChartSummaries` writes from the same rows (such as "44 sales over the last twelve months; the most, 6, in August 2026."), which the caption repeats for sighted readers. There is no chart script, no inline style and no `RawHtml`. `DashboardWords` writes counts with their noun and whole dollars.

## Live updates

The `LiveParts` element carries `data-poll__5s="/app"`. Every five seconds Tessera 0.3.0 sends one ordinary request for the component around it, which is the `LiveParts` itself, marked on that element: a `fetch` GET of `/app` with `Tessera-Request: refresh` and a `Tessera-Targets` entry of the part's path, its props and, under protocol 2 (AIC #194), its anchor last. The part seals its version in its props (`data-t-props`, AIC #193): the vehicles' count and latest `updated_at`, the sales' count and latest `created_at`, the customers' latest `updated_at` and the database's today (`DashboardRepository::version()`; staff names are left out, since `users` keeps no time of change). Tessera sends the props back with each refresh. `DashboardPage` reads the version first and asks `TesseraProps::unchanged()`, by the anchor `@dashboard-live`, whether the request is a refresh of the live part alone carrying that version; then it answers `PageUnchanged`, a 204 with no body, before it builds anything, and Tessera leaves the page as it is. Otherwise it builds the document, and AIC's `PageHandler` answers the refresh with that one target as a fragment, the tiles and the feed with it, the version sealed again; Tessera morphs them in place, and the element, whose attribute stays the same, keeps its clock. The charts are sources too, so a refresh reads none of their figures. Any request that sends altered props for the live part answers 400, whatever else it names.

The live part is the panel grid's first item, before the three charts (#15). Under protocol 2 a keyed component carries its props token wherever it renders, a template's child included, and Tessera sends the anchor with every entry, so `DashboardPoll` reads the props by `@dashboard-live` and nothing in the module spells or composes a path. From #14 until #15 the part led `content` as a child of the region itself, outside the grid, because only a region's child carried props then.

Tessera skips a tick while the tab is hidden or the browser is offline, and while the window has no focus it sends only every twentieth. No request stays open and no worker is held for an open dashboard; a visit away from the page takes the element, and its poll, with it. A sale recorded or voided anywhere shows within one tick. The part carries the anchor `dashboard-live` (AIC #188, `DashboardLive::ANCHOR`), printed as `data-t-anchor` on that element, so the tests and the native step name it as `@dashboard-live` and no code spells its path. `LiveParts` spans the grid's twelve columns and lays its parts out on twelve of its own, so the tiles and the wide panel sit on the grid's columns, `gap-6` from the charts. The interval is written in the theme's template, since an attribute's name cannot come from a property.

Until #12 the two parts were patched over a Tessera stream at `/app/events`, which held one worker of the compiled site for every open dashboard while it looked at the sales once a second.

## Figures

The reports are views the dashboard's own migration, `CreateDashboardViews`, creates with `Schema::view()` (#14, AIC #191): `dashboard_tiles`, `dashboard_months`, `dashboard_makes` and `dashboard_salespeople`. Their SQL, over the `vehicles`, `sales` and `users` tables the modules it requires own, is the one place the module writes any. `DashboardRepository` reads each through a read-only entity type named as a literal, the latest sales through sales' exported `SaleRepository::latest()` (joined to the vehicle, the customer and the salesperson through the sale's references), and the version through the entity types inventory and customers export, `SaleRepository::version()` and inventory's `TodayRepository`; it writes nothing. Every view counts against the database's `current_date`, so no date is worked out in PHP and both of gate:23's sites read the same day: "this month" is the database's calendar month (`to_char(…, 'YYYY-MM')`), and "the last twelve months" start on the first day of the month eleven before it, as old Lotline's ticket 9 counted them. The months come from `generate_series`, left-joined to their sales, so a month without one counts 0.

| Method | What it reads |
|---|---|
| `tiles()` | the vehicles `in_stock` and `on_hold`, the vehicles not `sold` and `round(avg(current_date - added_on))` over them, and the count and price sum of this month's and last month's sales, in one row (`TileFigures`) |
| `months()` | each of the twelve months, the oldest first: its short name, its note (the year, on the first month and on January), its full name and its count of sales (`MonthSales`) |
| `makes()` | the vehicles not sold per make (`Figure`) |
| `salespeople()` | each salesperson's price sum since the twelve months began (`Figure`) |
| `latest(int)` | the sales recorded last, by id, the latest first, with the vehicle's title and the customer's and salesperson's names (sales' `Sale`) |
| `version()` | what the live part's figures are now, as above |

A row off `rows()` is read through a method with a native `Row` parameter, as `SaleRows` does, since elephc reads no phpdoc.

## Checks

`config/native-site.json` ends with `GET /app` signed in (200), after the sales steps, and the staff steps still ask for it signed out (303) and signed in. Signed in, it then refreshes the live part, first by its path and, since #13, by `Tessera-Targets: @dashboard-live`, which each site resolves from its own page; both sites answer each with the same fragment. Since #14 a step refreshes the makes chart alone by `@dashboard-makes`. A step sends no props, so each of these renders; the 204 needs props the page sealed, which the browser look holds.

The browser looks `tests/browser/dashboard-tiles.spec.ts` and `tests/browser/dashboard-charts.spec.ts` sign in through `/app`, put a vehicle in stock and one sold today (`0LTPWDSH…` VINs, to `pw.dashboard@example.test`) in the database through `psql`, and compare the greeting, every tile with its line, and each chart's title, summary and bars or columns with what their own SQL gives; the tiles look also deletes its sale and sees this month's figures drop by it. `tests/browser/support/dashboard.ts` holds the rows, their cleanup and those queries.

`tests/browser/dashboard-poll.spec.ts` holds the poll element to `/app`, with no `data-stream` on the page, then records a sale through the form in a second page of the same session and sees it first in the feed and counted in the tiles within eight seconds (one tick and its request), with no reload and no document loaded; every request the page sent meanwhile is a finished refresh of the part, by the path `anchorPath()` finds from its anchor, answered with that fragment alone or, while nothing changed, 204, and none goes to `/app/events`. `tests/browser/dashboard-poll-idle.spec.ts` holds the 204: while nothing changes each poll answers it and the page stays; after a sale the next poll answers the live part alone and the charts keep the markup they had. `tests/browser/dashboard-poll-hidden.spec.ts` reports the page hidden through an init script, since headless Chromium never hides a page: through two ticks no refresh goes out and a sale put in Postgres does not show; shown again, the next tick brings it. `tests/browser/support/live.ts` holds their rows (`0LTPWLIV…` VINs, `pw.live@example.test`), the queries and `recordPolls()`, which takes each answer through a route, since Tessera cancels a refresh's body once it has read it and Chrome may then report the request aborted.
