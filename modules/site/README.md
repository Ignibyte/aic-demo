# Lotline foundation

The homepage introduces Lotline for the dealership its profile names (fictional Ridgeway Motors until an admin renames it, #23). It describes the forthcoming dealership operations app and links to the existing database health API. Staff sign in through the `staff` module, keep the lot through the `inventory` module, the customers through the `customers` module and the sales through the `sales` module; signed-in staff land on the `dashboard` module's page at `/app`.

`BootstrapPage` puts the theme's `TopBar` in the `header` region and its `BootstrapIntro` in `content`, through `PageDocuments`, titled `Dashboard · Lotline` after its heading and the signed-out sidebar's link to it. Its health link carries `data-enhance="off"`, since `/health` answers JSON, which Tessera cannot swap in: a click loads it as a document. Two blocks fill the rest of the drawer on every page: `NavigationBlock` places the sidebar (the site's name, then a link per area with its Heroicon, the current one marked from the request path; areas join as their pages land, and Inventory, Customers, Sales, Leads and Holds (#20, its clock icon) show only when `StaffSession`, which `site` requires `staff` for, holds a signed-in member, Reports (#21, its chart-bar icon) only when that member is an admin or a sales manager, who read the reports, and Settings (#22, the cog) only when they are an admin, the role staff's `StaffStanding` keeps current in the session; for that member Dashboard goes to the dashboard at `/app` (#8), current on `/app` alone since every staff page sits under it, and otherwise to `/`) and `SiteFooterBlock` the footer line, naming the dealership through staff's `StaffDealership`. The `header` region is a row: the page's `TopBar`, then the `staff` module's `AccountMenuBlock`. The components live in `themes/lotline/`: each template in `components/<Name>/`, each class in `src/Components/<Name>/`.

`ThemeGuidePage` answers `/theme`, the style guide, from `ThemeSamples`, which composes the sample services: charts, records (the list honours `q`, `status`, `sort` and `page`), filters, stock, forms, actions and parts. `ThemeGuidePost` checks the sample vehicle form (`ThemeEntry`) and answers 422 with each field's error, or 303 back to the guide (both pages are titled `Theme · Lotline`); the delete and sign-out samples go back too. Nothing is stored. The sample account menu passes its own id stem, `sample-account`, and its own form ids, so `/theme` signed in holds each id once beside the header's menu. `ThemeSampleStock::tone()` and `ThemeEntry::problems()` avoid `match` over enum cases and `array_intersect_key` until the compiler carries AIC #164 and #165 land.

The framework serves `/assets/aic.css` (the bundled theme stylesheet), the pinned Tessera script and its map from `build/assets/`, with byte ranges, before the application is assembled. The native comparison steps cover health, a missing route, the homepage, those files, missing and traversal asset requests and a stylesheet range.

The site has no brand icon yet: browser requests for `/favicon.ico` return 404.
