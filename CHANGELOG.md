# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Reports and exports (#21), in the `reports` module, which now requires `customers` too. `GET /app/reports` gives a manager (an admin or a sales manager) four reports over a range: sales by month, revenue and units by salesperson, inventory by make with average days on the lot, and leads by kind with how many became a sale. Each has a theme chart, a table and an "Export CSV" button. The range is this month unless `from` and `to` name another; presets for last month, this quarter and this year come from the database, and a GET form takes two days. Both refresh the content in place through Tessera and work with JavaScript off; a bad range answers 422 beside its field. Every figure comes from views read through AIC's totals with the range as conditions, so PHP works out no date. AIC #209 adds `CsvDocument` and `EntityTotals::averageBy()`, AIC #211 fixes `sumBy()` on an Integer field. An export is stored and its `report-export` job queued in one transaction. The job writes the report's rows to `var/exports/{id}.csv` and tells the member, kept and mailed. `GET /app/reports/exports/{id}` downloads it as `text/csv` under its report's and range's name, honouring ranges, for that member alone (another is 404); the `prune-exports` schedule removes exports past seven days. `GET /api/reports/sales` answers the sales report as JSON, with 401, 403 and 422 as JSON. A salesperson sees no Reports link and gets 403 in the staff frame. The theme gains `RangeLinks` and `DownloadLink`, whose `download` attribute keeps Tessera off a CSV. The inbox's opening answers a full visit, since a notification's link may be a file to save, and gains `bin/aic inbox:forget {login}` (AIC #210's `Inbox::forget()`), which gate:23 runs for its own user before each pass and after its export's worker step (AIC #209's `"work"`, AIC #210's `"console"`), so both passes show that inbox alike.
