# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The dashboard (#8), in a new `dashboard` module that requires `inventory`, `sales` and `staff`. `GET /app` moves to it from `staff`, whose `StaffHomePage` is gone, and the greeting it showed moves into the header: the theme's `TopBar` takes an optional line under its title ("Admin at Ridgeway Motors"), and `StaffPages::page()` passes it on. The page gives four of the theme's `StatTiles` (vehicles in stock with those on hold, sales and revenue this month with last month's, the average days on the lot of the vehicles not sold) and three charts drawn as SVG on the server by the theme's `ColumnChart` and `BarChart`: sales by month over the last twelve months, the vehicles in stock or on hold by make, and revenue by salesperson over the twelve months. Each chart has its title and a text summary in its SVG for screen readers, its colours from the stylesheet's classes; there is no chart script, inline style or `RawHtml`. Every figure is counted in SQL against the database's today. Signed in, the sidebar's Dashboard link goes to `/app`. One native step and two Playwright tests that compare every tile and bar with Postgres cover it; the sign-in look reads the greeting in the header.
- Sales (#7), in a new `sales` module that requires `inventory`, `customers` and `staff`. A `sales` table (the vehicle, unique, so it is sold once; the customer and the salesperson; the price in whole dollars; the day), with foreign keys that keep a sold vehicle, a buyer and a salesperson on the record. `GET /app/sales` lists the sales newest first, 15 a page, filtered by month and salesperson by plain GET parameters, with the totals of what the filters keep (how many, the revenue and the gross profit) in the theme's stat tiles; the sidebar links to it for signed-in staff. `GET /app/sales/new` (or "Record a sale" on a vehicle's page, which chooses the vehicle and fills its list price) and `POST /app/sales` record a sale: a vehicle on the lot that is not sold, a customer, a member of staff, whole dollars and a real day neither in the future nor before the vehicle came; a refusal answers 422 in place. Recording marks the vehicle sold in the same transaction, with its row locked, so a second sale of it answers 422 too. Each sale has a page at `/app/sales/{id}`, and `GET …/{id}/void` asks before `POST …/{id}/void` removes it and puts the vehicle back in stock (its form sealed in AIC's envelope). The vehicle's page shows its sale, and its edit keeps the status sold while the sale stands; the customer's page lists what they bought and counts it in its badge, and a customer who bought cannot be deleted (422). Inventory and customers learn of sales through plugin types they export (`VehicleSales`, `CustomerPurchases`), since they cannot require `sales`. `bin/aic sales:seed` saves 36 invented sold vehicles and 44 sales over the twelve months to October 2026, the same on every run. No theme component is added. Twenty-four native steps and four Playwright tests cover it.
- Customers (#6), in a new `customers` module that requires `staff`. A `customers` table (name, email unique and stored in small letters, phone, address, notes) and `bin/aic customers:seed`, which saves 40 invented customers at `@example.test` with 555-01xx phones, the same on every run. `GET /app/customers` lists them by name, 15 a page, searched over name, email and phone, every control a plain GET parameter; the sidebar links to it for signed-in staff. Each customer has a page at `/app/customers/{id}` with their details and the vehicles they bought (empty until sales exist); `GET …/new` and `POST /app/customers` add one, `GET …/{id}/edit` and `POST …/{id}` change one, and `GET …/{id}/delete` asks before `POST …/{id}/delete` removes one. The form needs a name and a valid email no other customer has, checks a given phone and the address's and notes' lengths, and answers a refusal with 422, each error beside its field and every value as typed; a save answers 303 to the customer with a notice. The edit and delete forms seal the customer's path in AIC's signed envelope (400 when it is missing, altered or another customer's). gate:23 reaches the created customer through `captures` and `{note}` (AIC #178). The record pages' panel, their one-time notice and the envelope now live once in `staff` (`StaffPanels`, `StaffNotices`, `StaffSeals`), and `inventory` uses them in place of its own copies, with no change in its behaviour. No theme component is added. Twenty native steps and five Playwright tests cover it.
- A vehicle's record (#5), in the `inventory` module. Each vehicle has a page at `/app/inventory/{vin}` (its VIN, not its id, so gate:23's two passes agree on its path), linked from the list, which also offers "Add a vehicle". `GET /app/inventory/new` and `POST /app/inventory` add one, `GET …/{vin}/edit` and `POST …/{vin}` change one, and `GET …/{vin}/delete` asks before `POST …/{vin}/delete` removes one; a sold vehicle cannot be deleted (422). The form checks the VIN's format, ISO 3779 check digit and uniqueness, a model year from 1981 to next year, the texts' lengths, whole numbers from 0, a known status and a real date, and answers a refusal with 422, each error beside its field and every value as typed; a save answers 303 to the vehicle with a notice. The edit and delete forms seal the vehicle's path in AIC's signed envelope, and a post sealed for another vehicle, or altered, answers 400. The pages use the theme's `FormGrid`, `FormField`, `SelectField`, `FormActions`, `DetailCard`, `ConfirmCard` and `Notice`; no component is added. Twenty native steps and five Playwright tests cover it.
- The inventory list (#4), in a new `inventory` module that requires `staff`. A `vehicles` table (VIN unique, what the vehicle is, mileage, colour, list price and cost in whole dollars, status, the date added) and `bin/aic inventory:seed`, which saves 60 invented vehicles across twelve makes, the same on every run, with fictional VINs (`0LT…`) that carry a valid check digit. `GET /app/inventory` shows them to signed-in staff, 15 a page, with a search over make, model and VIN, filters for make, status and a price range, sorting by column (a second click reverses it; `aria-sort` on the sorted head) and pages, every control a plain GET parameter that falls back to its default when it is unknown or malformed. The sidebar links to it for signed-in staff. `staff` exports `StaffPages` and `StaffSession`, and `site` requires it. `SignedInMenu` takes an id stem, so the `/theme` sample no longer repeats the header's ids. Nine native steps (a signed-in `/theme` among them) and four Playwright tests that compare the list with Postgres cover it.
- Staff sign in (#3), the first product feature, in a new `staff` module on AIC's Auth and sessions. A `users` table (email unique, name, role, pgcrypto bcrypt hash) with three roles: admin, sales manager and salesperson. `GET /sign-in` and `POST /sign-in` (back to where the visitor was going, kept on the site, else `/app`; a wrong email or password answers 422 without saying which), `POST /sign-out`, and `GET /app`, a first page for signed-in staff that greets them by name and role. Everything under `/app` sends a signed-out visitor to sign in and back. The header shows the signed-in menu (name, role, sign-out) or a "Sign in" link, beside the top bar. `bin/aic user:add {login} {password}` makes an admin; `bin/aic staff:seed` saves the admin and invented staff from `LOTLINE_*` settings. Seven native steps and four Playwright tests in `tests/browser/` cover the flow.
- AIC's new layout (AIC #167), with no change in behaviour. The theme's PHP moves out of its template folders: component classes to `themes/lotline/src/Components/<Name>/`, the page data to `themes/lotline/src/Layouts/Page.php`, with the same class names and namespaces. The Tailwind build moves from the root `frontend/` to `themes/lotline/frontend/` and still writes the same `lotline.css`. Everything the build writes is under `build/`: `build/generated/` in place of `generated/`, `build/assets/` in place of `public/assets/`, and `public/` holds only `index.php`. AIC moves to main b74fb24, and the suite stubs follow it.
- The rest of Lotline's theming (AIC #128, slice 2): form fields, selects, form grid and actions, filter bar, data table with sortable heads and badge cells, status badges, record and confirm cards, notices, pagination, stat tiles, panels, column and bar charts (old Lotline's geometry, computed by theme builders), the signed-in menu and the remaining Heroicons, all as typed theme components. `/theme` shows them with sample data, its list filtered, sorted and paged in place, its form answered by a real 422. The sidebar links Theme.
- Lotline's theme, `themes/lotline/` (AIC #128): old Lotline's look on current AIC. Its two daisyUI themes (dark the default) and atom styling, built by Tailwind 4 from `frontend/` into the theme's assets; the drawer shell as `.phtml` layouts with `sidebar`, `header`, `content` and `footer` regions; the top bar, sidebar navigation with Heroicons (each a template branch) and the footer as theme components, placed by the site module's page and blocks. AIC moves to main 74cd852; the suite stubs and the Codex workflow pack are current. The site module holds no component, template or asset, and the framework serves assets in place of the module's own controller.
